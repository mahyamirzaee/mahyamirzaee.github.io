*From Loneliness to Belonging: Understanding the Needs of Immigrant Women in Germany and Designing an LLM-Based Companion for (emotional) Support.* M.Sc. thesis in Human-Computer Interaction, University of Siegen, May 2026.

## The question

Research has examined AI-based support systems and community-based forms of belonging, but mostly separately. Less is known about how people understand and navigate the two in relation to each other in everyday life. My thesis asks:

> How do recently arrived Farsi-speaking immigrant women from Iran interpret and negotiate AI-mediated emotional support during early migration, and what tensions emerge in this process?

## How I worked

- **8 semi-structured narrative interviews** in Farsi, each 35 to 88 minutes long
- **Inductive–abductive thematic analysis**, following Braun and Clarke
- **Five structured tensions** as the analytical frame
- **Composite characters and analytical design fictions**, to make the tensions tangible without exposing any participant
- **A clickable conceptual prototype**, SafeSpace

## Five tensions

The women used AI as a low-threshold resource for reflection, practical guidance, and coping with uncertainty. At the same time, they stayed aware of its limits and kept valuing human relationships, cultural resonance, and community. Between these two sides, five tensions appeared:

1. **Uncertainty and orientation**
2. **Expression and self-protection**
3. **Digital clarity and relational depth**
4. **Continuity and data control**: being remembered, and being tracked
5. **Warmth and artificiality**

## Design fictions

I wrote five short scenarios with composite characters. Each shows how one tension unfolds in everyday life, for example deciding how much of an official letter to share with an AI. The fictions are an analytical tool: they keep the findings close to lived situations while protecting the people who shared them.

## SafeSpace

SafeSpace is a mobile-first web app and a conceptual prototype. It has three areas:

- **A private reflection space** to put thoughts into words
- **Shared anonymous experiences**, to read that others have been through something similar
- **A support directory** that points to real help nearby

It also includes privacy settings and right-to-left support for Farsi.

> AI support becomes meaningful not by imitating human closeness, but by being bounded, low-threshold, and under the user’s control: one layer next to human and community support, not a replacement.

## Contribution

The thesis offers empirical insights into AI-mediated emotional support in migration, a framework of structured tensions, and design implications for culturally sensitive and ethically grounded AI systems.
