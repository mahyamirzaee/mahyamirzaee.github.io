Before HCI, my work was material. During my B.A. in Textile Design at the Art University of Tehran and in my own design studio, Maya Studio (2016–2021), I made hand-woven textiles and commissioned branding, textile, and visual design projects, from concept to production.

## Material exploration

I see this work as an exploration of material: structure, pattern, colour, texture, and how a material feels in the hand. This way of looking still shapes how I approach interaction design and research.
