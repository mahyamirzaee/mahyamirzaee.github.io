A qualitative interview study with 16 people who believe they were deceived by scam chatbots.

## What we found

People noticed that they were talking to a chatbot through:

- **Repetitive messages**
- **Immediate or perfectly timed replies**
- **Emotional flatness**
- **Failure to handle off-topic questions**
