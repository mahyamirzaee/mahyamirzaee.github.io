CrossComITS is a BMBF-funded research project at the University of Siegen on participatory digital learning and private IT security for vulnerable groups.

## Context

In Smartphone Café sessions, older adults learn to use their smartphones safely, supported by mediators. The study is based on six months of ethnographic fieldwork in two small towns in western Germany.

## What we found

Safer behaviour grew out of four mediating practices:

- **Trusted accompaniment**
- **Slow-paced security onboarding**
- **Peer guardianship**
- **Discursive reframing**

Security learning turned out to be social, not only technical.

## My role

As a student research assistant, I facilitated sessions and workshops, carried out observations and interviews, worked on the qualitative analysis, and supported the literature review for the publication.
