In a summer internship at Robofinder, I worked on the user experience of a B2B platform, from qualitative research to high-fidelity prototypes.

## Research

I conducted qualitative interviews with partner agencies and synthesised their usability feedback into a prioritised UX roadmap.

## Design

- **Redesign** of the hero section, the navigation architecture, and the conversion funnels, to improve B2B onboarding and lead generation
- **Wireframes** of interactive user journeys
- **High-fidelity prototypes** of discovery-to-conversion flows, built with Claude Code

## Content and quality

I audited the site's UX, optimised the structure of the CMS blog, and refined the English localisation and copywriting.
