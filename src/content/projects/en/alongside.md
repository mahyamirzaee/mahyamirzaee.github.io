A UX internship project: a platform that helps businesses compare and buy service robots, redesigned from four user interviews and partner-agency feedback. The company is anonymised; the name, the makers and all robot data are invented.

The needs check moved to the top of the page, every question takes several answers, and the result counts each robot against the buyer's own seven criteria — met, not met, or unknown, with the unknown shown instead of left blank. One question was added: who works next to the robot? The result answers it with a short note for that team.

The full project page, with the research findings, the tensions and a reflection, is at [mahyamirzaee.github.io/alongside](https://mahyamirzaee.github.io/alongside/).
