Shared Threads is my first physical computing exploration. It starts from my own hand-woven textile, 50 × 20 cm, with two hidden touch zones and an ESP32 board inside. When two people touch the two ends at the same time, light glows in the fringes.

## The question

Can a soft, tactile object create a small moment of shared attention between strangers in public space?

## Next ideas

- **Warmth** that appears where both hands rest
- **Gentle vibration** as a quieter signal than light
- **Light travelling along the stripes**, from one hand to the other
